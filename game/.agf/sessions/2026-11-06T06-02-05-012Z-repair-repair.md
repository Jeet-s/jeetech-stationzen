**level_replay.** The replay code itself works: on the emulator, the level 1 deep link logged the first move within 0.5 s, both on a fresh install and with saved data. The likely cause is the shared emulator. While I held the emulator lock, the other factory's QA apps (`com.jeetech.podium`, `com.jeetech.fluentfriend`) took the foreground three times. That pushes Station Zen into the background, and a backgrounded app reports nothing. To make the game survive that anyway, `GameScreen` now:
- retries the move report until it goes out;
- re-sends it when the app comes back to the foreground;
- falls back to the board position measured at layout if the fresh measurement never returns.

**render_defects** (checked on the emulator)
- **Root cause:** every station's demand chip sat on the side of the station its rails leave from. The tutorial hand, the line-end knobs and the trains all travel along those rails, so they covered the chips.
- **Chips:** each chip now goes on the station's free side, never off the board and never over another station.
- **Tutorial hand:** it rides beside the path instead of along it. Its guide ribbon is drawn under the labels, and it loops 5 times instead of 90.
- **Rider count:** it stops at the goal and shows "✓ +2" as a bonus, so it no longer reads "21 / 19". The win card says "Goal 19 reached · +2 bonus riders".

**game_feel**
