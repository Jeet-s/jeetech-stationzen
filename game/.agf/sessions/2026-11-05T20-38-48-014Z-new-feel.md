Stage 3 is finished. `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npm run pitfalls` (0 errors, 0 warnings) and `npm run validate` (120 of 120 levels solvable) all pass. I checked the new screens in a web preview at 360×640 and 390×844, so device QA will be their first run on a real phone. I didn't change any levels, so I didn't rerun playtest or the level review.

**Modes that were missing after stage 2 (online play is required for this game)**
- **Online Duels** (`OnlineScreen.tsx`, `DuelScreen.tsx`), opened from a Duel button on Home:
  - **Quick Duel:** both players plan the same city map, and the most riders delivered wins.
  - **AI rival:** if nobody is found in about 10 seconds, or the phone is offline, Pip's AI rival plays the level's proven plan with a random skill factor.
  - **Friend Room:** create a room and share its code, or join one by typing a code (letters and digits only).
  - **During and after:** a progress strip shows both players' riders, with fixed emotes only and a mute button. The result card shows both scores, the Tokens earned counting up and the rating change.
  - **Ranking:** a duel-rating leaderboard and a "How matchmaking works" note.
  - **Rules:** a rival who leaves before the start cancels the duel with no result; one who leaves mid-duel gives you the win. Every network call gives up after at most 12 seconds and falls back to the AI or a clear message.
- **Daily Puzzle** (`DailyScreen.tsx`), opened from a Daily button on Home:
  - One map a day, the same for every player.
  - A 7-day streak ribbon, plus one free streak freeze a week.
  - Tokens for the win, an opt-in evening reminder, and a staged win card.
- **Station Garden** (`GardenScreen.tsx`), opened from a Garden icon on Home:
  - A paper diorama island cut from the city map art, with 12 pieces bought with Tokens and unlocked by stars.
  - Bought pieces pop onto their spot with a burst and bob gently; empty spots show as dashed outlines.

**Game feel**
- **Deliveries:** the riders counter counts up and pops, and a "+N riders" floats up.
- **Calm-week streak:** calm weeks in a row build a "Smooth ×N" streak with rising chimes.
- **While a week runs:** each station on a line chimes with sparks in turn.
- **Refused moves:** the bottom tray gives a gentle wobble. The map itself never shakes, as the art direction asks.
- **Undo, redo and card picks:** each now shows a burst.
- **Level map:** Pip sits on the current level, and a tapped level bursts before it opens.

**Fixed along the way**
The duel AI rival first replayed the stored winning plans the wrong way. That crashed on 20 of the 120 levels. It now replays them the way `validate` does, and all 120 work.
