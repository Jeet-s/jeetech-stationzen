**level_design**
- **What changed:** every listed level was redesigned per its fix. Levels 1 and 2 now have a "deliver every rider" 3rd star, a new star type in the engine. The levels the review named after the first round were reworked too, and the level plan (`docs/LEVELS.md`), the level descriptions and the stored solutions are all updated.
- **Why it won't converge:** the review keeps asking for gentler late levels, but the difficulty curve that `npm run validate` requires (late levels at least 3× early ones) is nearly at its limit. It's at 3.09×. Easing one late level means raising goals on others, which then get flagged as too tight.

**Factory issue:** the level review and the validate difficulty curve pull against each other on this 120-level set, and the review's verdict changes a lot from run to run. Suggested fix: calibrate the reviewer against the curve, or average a few runs before failing a build. The "≥3 rounds failing only on quality" ship rule may apply here.
