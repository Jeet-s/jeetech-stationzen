# Station Zen — store facts (only what the code does)

Summary (details below):
- 120 handcrafted levels in 10 cities; draw metro lines, every week starts paused to plan — src/game/levels.ts, src/game/engine.ts
- Station Garden: decorate a paper diorama island by placing 12 pieces bought with Tokens, unlocked by stars — src/screens/GardenScreen.tsx, src/game/modes.ts GARDEN
- Daily Puzzle with streaks; Online Duels with AI fallback; Season Pass (free track); City Mastery skins; Passenger Journal — src/screens/*
- Ads: interstitials show automatically at level ends (first after level 10, then at most one every 4 levels; never during a level); banner only after level 5; only rewarded videos are player-chosen; Remove Ads purchase turns off interstitials and banners — src/modules/ads.ts

Details:

- Campaign: 120 handcrafted levels in 10 cities of 12 (Willowmere, Portside Bay, Kitebrook, Amberhill, Lotus Delta, Frostholm, Sunbazaar, Ringvale, Neon Quay, Grand Meridian) — src/game/chapters/*.ts, src/game/levels.ts
- Core rule: draw metro lines along rail corridors; passengers ride to the station with the glyph they want and change lines at shared stations — src/game/engine.ts
- Pause and plan: every week starts frozen; edit, undo (last 3 edits of the week), then run the week — src/game/engine.ts, src/game/sim.ts
- Soft fail: station rings go yellow → amber → full ("Last Call"); a run ends only if a station stays full 2 weeks in a row — src/game/engine.ts
- Mechanics introduced one city at a time: river tunnels, weekly card pick (+line / +2 tracks / +tunnel / +carriage / +interchange / calm), carriages, hills, ferry links, snow weeks, interchanges, market days, loop lines, one-way rails, rush-hour waves, heritage stations — src/game/engine.ts, docs/LEVELS.md
- Stars: 1★ deliver the goal by the last week, 2★ deliver more, 3★ the level's special goal (deliver every rider / no amber ring / tracks to spare / line limit / loop / nobody left waiting) — src/game/engine.ts starsFor
- 8 passenger glyphs: leaf, drop, kite, lantern, moon, key, cup, star — src/game/engine.ts GLYPHS
- Every level is proven winnable by a solver (stored plan replayed by scripts/validate.ts) — src/game/solutions.json
- Controls: drag from a station to a neighbouring station to draw a line, drag a line's end knob to extend it (or onto its other end to close a loop), drag the carriage / interchange chip onto a line / station; tap a station for its demand card — src/screens/GameScreen.tsx, src/game/board.ts
- Station demand card: riders per week per wanted glyph, waiting count, ring stage, forecast week it fills, and a route preview that highlights the lines its riders take — src/screens/GameScreen.tsx StationCard
- Never a surprise: while planning, a forecast chip shows projected deliveries vs the goal, and stations that would fill show a dashed amber ring and a "full wk N" tag — src/game/engine.ts forecast, src/screens/game/MapOverlays.tsx
- Undo and redo the last 3 edits of the week; restart the level; after a soft fail, retry from the start of the last week (optionally with a Breather via an opt-in video) — src/screens/GameScreen.tsx
- Weekly card pick: 2-3 cards (+1 line, +2 tracks, +1 tunnel, +1 carriage, +1 interchange, calm: halve every queue), with "View map first" — src/screens/GameScreen.tsx, src/game/meta.ts
- Boosters: Hint (shows the next winning move with a hand) and Breather (halves every waiting queue); 3 Hints + 2 Breathers to start, more for Tokens in the Shop or via an opt-in video; never cash-only — src/screens/ShopScreen.tsx
- Tokens (soft currency): 10 + 10 per star (+2 per city) for each win; Shop: Hint ×3 for 90, Breather ×3 for 120, free 40 Tokens per opt-in video, Remove Ads (always listed on Android, store price) and Token packs as store purchases, Restore purchases — src/game/meta.ts rewardFor, src/screens/ShopScreen.tsx
- First-time tutorial on levels 1-2 (Pip the station cat + a hand showing each drag/tap, Skip tutorial always visible; replayable any time from Settings → "Replay tutorial (level 1)") and a one-time tip card the first time each rule appears (15 rules) — src/screens/GameScreen.tsx, src/ui/SettingsPopup.tsx, src/game/meta.ts tipsFor
- Saved runs: leaving mid-level resumes at the start of the current week — src/screens/GameScreen.tsx (store.run)
- Accessibility: colour-blind line patterns (solid, dashed, dotted, double, dash-dot, long dash), reduce motion; Sound, Music, Vibration toggles; opt-in daily reminder — src/ui/SettingsPopup.tsx, src/screens/game/MapCanvas.tsx
- 10 city looks (three illustrated maps with per-city colour washes) and a level map of all 120 levels with stars — src/game/meta.ts CITY_LOOK, src/screens/LevelsScreen.tsx
- Daily Puzzle: one proven map a day (cities 2-10), the same for every player on a UTC date; 50 Tokens + 10 per streak day (up to 7); a 7-day streak ribbon, best streak, and one free streak freeze per week that covers a missed day; opt-in evening reminder — src/game/modes.ts dailyLevel/completeDaily, src/screens/DailyScreen.tsx
- Online Duels: Quick Duel pairs you with a waiting player; both plan the same city map and the most riders delivered wins; if nobody is found in about 10 s (or offline) Pip's AI rival plays the level's proven plan with a skill factor; Friend Room by a shareable code; fixed emotes only (no chat, generated names), mute a rival; a rival leaving before the start cancels with no result — src/screens/OnlineScreen.tsx, src/screens/DuelScreen.tsx, src/game/modes.ts botWeeks
- Duel rating: +20 a win, -10 a loss (never below 0), purchases never change it; online leaderboard of duel ratings; 40 / 25 / 15 Tokens for a win / draw / loss — src/game/modes.ts recordDuel, src/screens/OnlineScreen.tsx
- Station Garden: a paper diorama island with 12 pieces (halt, interchange plaza, heritage hall, garden tram, riders, glyph lanterns, Pip's bench) bought with Tokens and unlocked by stars (0-120) — src/game/modes.ts GARDEN, src/screens/GardenScreen.tsx
- Game feel: a new connection fills with its line colour and glows, delivered riders fly into the rider counter (riders beyond the goal show as a bonus), stations pulse in their ring colour while a week runs, deliveries count up with a "+N riders" pop, calm weeks in a row build a "Smooth ×N" streak, stations chime with sparks as trains run, staged win (stations burst, confetti, stars, Tokens count up) — src/screens/GameScreen.tsx
- Heatmap Replay: a week-by-week replay of a run (every week re-simulated on the engine) with each station's end-of-week crowding as a coloured heat spot, a week scrubber and one tip for the busiest stop. Opened from the Home "Heatmap" button (your most recent finished run, saved on device; before any run has finished, a demo replay of level 1's winning plan labelled as a demo), from the win and soft-fail cards, and from a tray button in-game once a week has run — src/screens/game/Heatmap.tsx, src/screens/HomeScreen.tsx
- City Mastery & Skins: each of the 10 cities has a 5-tier mastery track (6 / 12 / 20 / 28 / 34 of its 36 stars); tiers unlock 6 line colour skins and 5 train liveries (plus the Classic ones) that recolour every map; opened from the Home "Skins" button, the Home city card or the Levels screen — src/game/collections.ts, src/screens/MetaPopups.tsx MasteryPopup
- Passenger Journal: 12 regular riders with story cards, each met by winning a level where a station of one glyph sends riders to another; opened from the Station Garden — src/game/collections.ts JOURNAL, src/screens/MetaPopups.tsx JournalPopup
- Account & Save (no account or password): progress saves on the phone; a backup code (letters and digits, offline, no network) restores level, stars, Tokens, boosters and garden on another phone; "Email me a sign-in code" puts the code in a message the player's own mail app sends to themselves (the game never stores or sends the address), then the code is entered on the new phone; in Settings — src/game/collections.ts backupCode/readBackup, src/screens/MetaPopups.tsx AccountPopup
- Season Pass (free track only): 8-week seasons with a theme; stars won this season fill 10 tiers of fixed, shown-in-advance rewards (Tokens, Hints, Breathers; a welcome gift at tier 1) claimed with a tap; opened from the Home "Season" button — src/screens/MetaPopups.tsx SeasonPopup, src/screens/HomeScreen.tsx
- Weekly card forecast: each card shows whether the goal stays in reach with it ("goal in reach" / "falls short"), computed by the game's solver — src/screens/GameScreen.tsx cardFc
