# Station Zen — level plan

The campaign is **120 handcrafted levels in 10 cities of 12**. Each city introduces one rule, practises it, twists it,
combines it with earlier rules, and ends on a boss and a gentle mastery level. Level data lives in
`src/game/chapters/*.ts` (one file per city). The per-level table at the end of this file is regenerated from that
data (`npx tsx scripts/plan-doc.ts`), so it always matches the game.

## How a level plays (engine: `src/game/engine.ts`)

- The map shows **stations** (a glyph plus the passengers it sends out each week) joined by **rail corridors**.
  The player draws **lines** along the corridors. Each corridor step costs one **track** from the week's budget,
  and a line can only grow from its two ends.
- Passengers ride to the nearest station with the glyph they want. They change lines at any shared station.
- A line's weekly seats are 6 × (1 + carriages) × 36 / (2 × hops). Short lines run often, and loops run twice as
  often. Riders who get no seat queue up at their station.
- **Rings:** a station's ring is yellow at half full, amber at ¾ and full at 12 riders (24 at an interchange). A full
  ring is "Last Call". If a station is still full the next week, the run ends softly. Heritage stations may never
  fill.
- **Win:** reach the last week having delivered the goal (a share of every rider). 2★ means delivering more.
  3★ is the level's special goal: no amber, tracks to spare, a line limit, a loop, nobody left waiting, or every
  rider delivered (used while overcrowding is off, so the tutorial star still means something).
- **Routing is visible (UI requirement for the screens stage):** riders pick the route with the fewest line changes,
  then the fewest hops. Tapping a station shows its demand card (riders per glyph, "0 riders" for pure destinations)
  and previews where its riders will ride, so levels built on routing (Morning Market L8, Wrong Pier L57) are fair.
- **Pause and plan:** every week starts frozen. The player edits, undoes, picks the weekly card and then presses
  Run. Time keeps flowing until something new happens: a station opens, tracks or a card arrive, the weather turns,
  or a ring rises a stage (the calm chime).
- Stations that open later are visible from the start as construction sites, so every level is fully readable.

## Pacing (bot playtest, `scripts/targets.ts` + `scripts/tune-levels.ts`)

The target casual first-try win rate is 0.90 on levels 1–3. After that it is `0.88 − 0.36 × (n−1)/119` plus a role
offset: teach +0.05, practise 0, twist −0.03, combine −0.05, boss −0.13, breather +0.06, mastery +0.04. The result
is a sawtooth that falls from about 90% to about 50%, with a boss every six levels and a breather after it. The tuner
only sets each level's goal share and demand scale; the map and its idea are handcrafted. Every level has a stored
winning plan (`src/game/solutions.json`, replayed by `npm run validate`). Most levels also have a **tempting wrong
plan** (`trap` in the level data) that the tools prove loses, which is the evidence that the level's aha is real.

## Aha variety (2026-11-06 redesign passes)

Not every level is "the obvious split is the trap". Each city mixes these aha types, and no more than two
levels in a row share one. The "keep a line END beside the station that opens later" template is used at most
once per city after Willowmere (where it is taught, L4): Save the Tunnel L18 / Lighthouse Rock L22, Toll Road L44,
Sea or River L58, Leave a Gap L88; the bosses and mastery levels that used to repeat it were rebuilt.
- **Line length vs load** (the classic): short lines run often (L3, L5, L10, L15, L20, L61).
- **Unequal load:** one stop outweighs the town and the network is shaped around it (The Sawmill L62, Night Market L20, Over the Hill L106).
- **Heavy pair / transfer design:** the stops that trade most share a line (Cross Streets L9, Two Huts L70, Spice Square L79, Lantern Market L103).
- **Hold back / spend less:** keep a track, a line or a card for later (Keep One Back L32, Keep a Line L50, Harbour Mastery L24, Opening Night L112), or leave a corridor unbuilt (Leave the Old Road L46).
- **Useless station / leave it unbuilt (inversions):** a pretty or central stop that serves nobody (Island Hop L17, The Short Ring L89, Ring on a Budget L94, Neon Rush Festival L107, Grand Meridian L120), or a ring that should stay open (Ferry Ring L92, Two Halves L101).
- **Budget economy:** count tracks and spend them where they double capacity (Ring on a Budget L94).
- **Routing / nearest glyph:** riders go to the nearest stop with their glyph, so a quiet stop can redirect a crowd (Two Keys L45, The Corner Kiosk L76, Wrong Pier L57).
- **Reading the forecast:** a one-week Last Call is survivable, a slow leak is not (One Loud Night L82, Bazaar Gate L78); build for the worst week (Neon Rush Festival L107: a wave meets a snow week).
- **Card judgement:** the first card is a free gift (L3); then cards cost a star at most (Harvest Festival L11, Harbour Mastery L24, Kitebrook Mastery L36, Frost Garden L67); from Card Day L16 on, the tempting card can lose (Card Day L16, Harvest Fair L42, Storm Opening L69, Grand Meridian L120), read against the map and the forecast.
- **Drawing puzzles (breathers):** the 3rd star is one line through every stop (Willow Pond L7, Amber Lanterns L43 over the hill temple).

**Fairness on big boards:** the factory report settles trap rates with a small search, so big boards show "trap rate ?".
`scripts/fairness.ts` checks EVERY first move of those levels on the real engine (breadth-first search, then a
guided rollout as a constructive proof) and stores the result in `solutions.json`; `describe()` quotes it
("all 8 first moves can still win (trap rate 0.00)").

## Repair pass 2026-11-06 (evening): curve, redesigns, new 3rd stars

- **Rest stops shrink late.** `scripts/targets.ts` now scales the positive role offsets (teach, breather, mastery)
  down as the campaign goes on (by up to 50-55% from L30 to L90): a late breather is a breather against ~55%
  neighbours (about 65-80% casual), not a free win. Hand-set targets: L47 0.70, L53 0.72, L88 0.72, L107 0.58,
  L120 0.50 (L120 was a 39% spike). Validate's difficulty now uses the casual bot's measured win rate plus the
  noisy search (`scripts/validate.ts`); late/early 3.35x, trend ρ 0.67.
- **New 3rd stars** (engine `Star3`): `nocar` "leave the carriage in the depot" (Kite Hill L31: the calm answer is
  two short lines, no carriage) and `direct` "these stops on one line" (Lantern Market L103: the heavy pair rides
  without a change — the aha is now rewarded).
- **Redesigned per review:** L40 Hilltop Stand (the flat lane is gone: one climb, one carriage), L54 Lotus Market
  (one decoy: the ferry line grown to the market; two honest card fixes), L83 Lantern Night Bazaar (the east market
  night is a snow week: the wrong card now loses; 3rd star = every rider), L88 Leave a Gap (hill removed: loops +
  a construction site only), L91 Lantern Pier (a hill on the pier: which way the pier line turns now matters, with a
  trap), L94 Ring on a Budget (the left-out kiosk sends a rider a week: a priced trade), L113 One Tunnel, Two Rings
  (both rings win, the heritage-side ring keeps all three stars; building no ring loses), L4 gets a harmless week-3
  card (the weekly card appears early in the campaign). L59 Lantern Regatta keeps its map; its text now states the
  truth: the week-1 carriage is repairable with the week-4 card, so the decision is the card, made with the fair on
  the map. (A one-tunnel L21 was tried and reverted: the nearest-glyph reasoning read as hidden information at L21.)
- Breathers L103 and L115 keep their gentler goals (real relief in the last third).
- **Clarified:** L16 (the card read on the map), L62 (one answer), L71 (one track with the festival: only one line
  can reach it), L76 (how to read the kiosk), L102 (the forecast shows the east line failing from week 1).
