Privacy Policy

Station Zen: Metro Builder (com.jeetech.stationzen) · JeeTech · last updated 2026-11-06

This policy explains what information Station Zen: Metro Builder (“the game”) and the services it uses collect, and how it is used.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location. Your game progress (level, coins, purchases unlocked) is stored only on your device.

Gameplay statistics

To balance level difficulty, the game sends anonymous gameplay events (for example “level 12 started”, “level 12 completed in 9 moves”) together with a random identifier created on your device at install. These events contain no name, email, advertising ID, device ID or location and cannot be used to identify you; they are kept for at most 90 days and are never sold or shared. Uninstalling the game deletes the random identifier.

Online play (Google Firebase)

For online matches, friend rooms and leaderboards the game signs you in anonymously with Google Firebase Authentication and stores your generated player name and avatar, an anonymous user ID, and your moves and scores in Google Firebase Realtime Database. Other players see only your generated name, avatar, game moves and preset emotes — there is no chat and no free text. This data is used only to run online play, is encrypted in transit, and is not linked to your identity. Match data is deleted automatically after a short time; leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboard runs. Google's handling is described in the Firebase privacy information.

Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

If you allow notifications, the game registers a push token from Google Firebase Cloud Messaging with our server, together with a random install identifier, so we can send reminders and game updates. No name, email or advertising ID is attached. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings; uninstalling the game removes the token.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), device and app information, and ad interaction data to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalise them. In the EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws we ask for your consent through Google's consent message before ads are requested; you can change your choice in the game or reset your advertising ID in Android settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details; Google shares only a purchase confirmation so the item can be delivered and restored. Google's handling is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Diagnostics

Google Play may provide us with anonymous crash and performance reports (Android vitals) that do not identify you.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with Google as described above, and is encrypted in transit.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices and deletion

Uninstalling the game deletes all game data stored on your device. You can opt out of personalised ads in your device settings. For any privacy request, contact us at the address below.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change; the date above shows the latest revision.
